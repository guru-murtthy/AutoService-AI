# AutoService AI - Commercial Strategy & Business Model

## Target Niche
**Travel agencies and car-rental/tour operators in India.**

## Initial Commercial Target
**₹5,000 / month recurring revenue** from first paying business.

## Tiered Subscription Model
- **STARTER (₹5,000 / month)**: AI enquiry handling, natural language requirement extraction, deterministic quotation engine, automated 3-tier follow-ups, CRM dashboard, daily business reports.
- **PRO (₹15,000 / month)**: Multi-agent automation, high-volume WhatsApp integration, custom vehicle rate tables, advanced profitability analytics.

## Unit Economics (Per Customer / Month)
- Subscription Revenue: ₹5,000
- AI API Cost (~100 enquiries @ ₹1.50/enquiry): ₹150
- Hosting & Infrastructure (Shared): ₹350
- **Net Contribution Margin**: ₹4,500 / month (90% Gross Margin)
